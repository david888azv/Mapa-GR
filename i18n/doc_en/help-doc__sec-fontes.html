Higher Education Census (microdata — 5 cycles)

The source behind the Higher Education Census 2017-2024 page. INEP publishes microdata aggregated by programme×institution with ~200 columns (gender, colour/race, age, time of day, FIES, PROUNI, quotas, disability, enrolment status and so on).

Ethnic-racial quotas in 2024: INEP discontinued the column for places reserved "on ethnic grounds" (valid from 2009 to 2023) and now splits it into Black, mixed-race and Indigenous (PPI), quilombola, and traditional peoples and communities. To keep the series, MAPA-GR uses the sum of the three in 2024 — 310,049 enrolments, against 306,423 in the old column in 2023.

The raw microdata (~570 MB in total, 457 MB for 2024 alone) are processed locally in Python/DuckDB to produce a light consolidated JSON (≈ 7 MB) that feeds the dashboard in the browser. The full pipeline is in the repository at censo_sup/pipeline_censo.py.
