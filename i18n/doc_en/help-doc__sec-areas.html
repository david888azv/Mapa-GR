The 9 broad fields

INEP's assessment fields (~67 distinct names) were mapped onto 9 broad fields compatible with the CAPES/CNPq classification. Each tab at the top of the screen is one broad field. Field names are kept in Portuguese, as they appear in the official data.

Notes on the mapping

  • Veterinary Medicine — Agricultural Sciences (consistent with CAPES/CNPq).
  • Psychology — Health Sciences (consistent with CAPES broad field 4).
  • Physical Education (teaching degree and bachelor) — Health Sciences.
  • Architecture and Urbanism — Engineering (its indicators behave alike).
  • Social Sciences (teaching/bachelor) — Humanities. "Applied Social Sciences" is the separate group with business, law and accounting.
  • Technologist programmes — short vocational degrees, kept in a tab of their own.
