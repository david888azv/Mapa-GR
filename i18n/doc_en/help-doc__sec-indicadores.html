How ENADE and IDD are assessed

Both indicators come from the same exam (ENADE), but they answer different questions: ENADE measures the exit level of graduating students, while the IDD measures the gain attributable to the programme, net of the level at which students arrived.

ENADE — National Student Performance Exam

What it measures: the absolute performance of graduating students — it answers "how well does this programme educate?".

  • An exam sat by the programme's final-year students (in the app, "registered/sat" refer to those students, not to total enrolment).
  • Applied on a three-year cycle by field (in MAPA-GR: the 2017, 2018, 2021, 2022 and 2023 cycles).
  • The exam combines General Education (25%) and a Field-Specific Component (75%).
  • Raw scores are standardised within the field (against the mean and standard deviation of programmes in the same field), yielding a continuous score that is converted into the 1 to 5 band (the ENADE score).
  • Insufficient participation → SC (no score).

IDD — Indicator of the Difference between Observed and Expected Performance

What it measures: the "value added" by the programme — how much it adds to the student, controlling for the quality of those who enrolled. It separates the programme's contribution from the selectivity of its intake.

  • For each graduating student, INEP estimates an expected ENADE performance from their ENEM entrance-exam score (proficiency on arrival), through a regression model.
  • IDD = observed ENADE performance − expected performance (the one predicted from ENEM).
  • Aggregated by programme, standardised within the field and converted into a continuous score plus a 1 to 5 band.
  • How to read it: a high IDD means graduates came out better than their starting point predicted — the programme "made the difference", whether its intake was strong or weak.
In one sentence: ENADE measures the exit level of graduating students; the IDD measures the gain attributable to the programme, net of the entry level (ENEM). That is why the IDD carries the largest weight in the CPC (35%), above ENADE itself (20%).
